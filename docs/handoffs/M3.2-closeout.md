## Verification

- Full checks:
  - ktlintFormat, ktlintCheck, assembleDebug and lintDebug (no issues) pass.
  - testDebugUnitTest: 1716 tests, 4 failures. Three are the known Home screenshot timeouts.
  - The fourth is `PlaybackControllerTest.enteringShuffleOrRepeatTrimsTheGeneratedTail` (D3b test, expected 2
    entries after shuffle, was 4). It passed 4 times in a row in isolation with `--rerun`, and in every earlier
    full run.
  - Suspected cause, not proven: `truncateAutoplayLocked` returns early when `currentPosition()` is briefly null
    right after a refill rebuilds the window under load, so shuffle starts with the tail. Every `readSnapshot()`
    caller holds `startLock`, so E3's extra label read can't interleave.
  - Worth hardening: retry truncation, or truncate from the cursor when the player has no current item.

- In the picker, a vertical swipe that starts on a genre column scrolls that column, not the page. That is
  normal nested scrolling, but scrolling the page means starting outside the columns.
- Home's Genres shelf still lists raw spellings (Jazz, techno, Techno, contemporary jazz); grouping it by the
  taxonomy would be a separate Home change.
