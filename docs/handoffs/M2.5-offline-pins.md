# M2.5 Part H: Pinned on offline Home

**Status:** complete (12 Nov 2026). With it, M2.5's pin work is done.

## Implemented
- Offline Home now starts with Pinned (after the title, before the downloaded shelves), in pin order, using the same
  carousel/grid choice and hidden setting as online Home. Tap and long press open the item and its menu, as online.
- Only pins that can play offline appear (`PinDao.downloadedPins`): an album with a completed song download, a playlist
  with a completed track download, or an artist with a completed download on one of their albums, by album artist or
  album credit (`DOWNLOADED_ARTIST_ROWS`, the rows the downloaded Artists list uses; that list's extra "credited"
  check is not applied, since a pinned artist was already shown somewhere).
  Queued or failed downloads don't count; another server's downloads don't count. Room only, paged.
- iOS shows every pin offline. Android keeps its offline rule (offline browsing shows downloaded content only, as on
  the Library lists and Favorites), so a pin never opens an empty page. Documented in FEATURE_PARITY.
- Pin reordering: iOS has a `PinService.reorder` method but no screen calls it; pins stay in pin order and the order
  compacts on unpin. So there is no reorder UI to match, and none was added. Android's position column already keeps
  pin order; re-pinning appends.
- Online and offline share `pinnedSection` and the pin click handlers. No schema, dependency or network change.
