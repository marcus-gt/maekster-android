# M2.5 Part B: Favorite albums on Home

**Status:** complete (12 Nov 2026). M2.5 remains in progress.

## Implemented

- Favorite albums shelf immediately after Your playlists on online Home.
- Up to 10 locally mirrored starred albums, newest star first, then sort name and ID.
- Album tap and long-press reuse the existing album card and menu.
- More opens a paged Home list using the existing starred album query and filters.
- Favorites-only feeds show Home rather than the empty sync prompt; empty shelves are omitted.
- No schema, dependency or playback changes. Offline Home remains the downloaded shelves.
- iOS HomeView / HomeFeedViewModel inspected for favorite shelf behavior; Android keeps local mirror reads.
