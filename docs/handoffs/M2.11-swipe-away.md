# M2.11 Part A: swipe-away keeps music playing

**Status:** DONE. Based on 8cec1564.

## Decision (C11, D8)

iOS keeps playing until the user force-quits. Android used to pause and stop the service whenever the
app was swiped from recents. Now music keeps playing; a paused, ended or empty player still stops the
service.

## Implementation

- `PlaybackService.onTaskRemoved` checks the session player through
  `keepPlayingOnTaskRemoved(playWhenReady, playbackState, mediaItemCount)` (top level, same file). It
  keeps the service when the user wants playback (READY or BUFFERING with playWhenReady) and there is
  something to play. Otherwise it calls `pauseAllPlayersAndStopSelf()` as before. It logs
  `playback: task removed: …`.
- Once the service is kept, Media3's foreground handling takes over: a later pause from the
  notification lets the system stop it as usual. The queue and position are already persisted, so the
  next launch restores them.

## Verification

- `TaskRemovedTest` (Robolectric, because `PlaybackService.kt` has a top-level `SessionCommand`):
  playing and buffering keep the service; paused, ended, idle and empty stop it.
- Mutation: an always-false decision (the old behaviour) fails the two "keep" tests. Restored.
- Emulator (API 37, Navidrome; scrobbling was turned off for the test and back on afterwards, so no
  server writes):
  - Playing, then swiped away: the log reads "still playing, keeping the service", the session stays
    PLAYING, the service stays in the foreground, and playback moved on to the next track in the
    background.
  - Relaunched, paused, then swiped away: the log reads "stopping", the service is neither started nor
    in the foreground, and the session stays PAUSED. The ServiceRecord stays while a controller is
    bound, the same as before. No crashes.
- Tip: in the emulator, `input touchscreen swipe 540 1500 540 200 120` in recents dismisses the card;
  slower swipes often don't.
- Checks: ktlintFormat, assembleDebug, ktlintCheck and lintDebug pass. The full suite ran 1590 tests
  with 4 failures: the known HomeScreenshotTest timeouts (3) and
  `SaveQueueViewModelTest.closingTheDialogAfterAFailureDiscardsTheCapture` (M2.9 code, untouched
  here). Both classes pass on their own (5/5 each). Watch the SaveQueueViewModelTest flake; it may be a
  timing race in the capture discard.

## Not verified

- Physical device and OEM task killers. Also process death while kept alive, which is covered by the
  "manual process-death test" in M2.11's restore part.
