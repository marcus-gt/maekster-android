## Implementation

- `data/musicbrainz/MusicBrainz.kt`:
  - `MusicBrainz` interface (`releaseGroups`, `artistId`) and `MbReleaseGroup` / `MbCredit` (`creditName`
    joins the credit with its join phrases).
  - `MusicBrainzRateLimiter`: one shared limiter, so all MusicBrainz features together stay at 1 req/s.
  - `OkHttpMusicBrainz`:
    - Its own OkHttp client: no server interceptors, credentials or headers; no redirects.
    - User-Agent is `UserAgent.value ( repository URL )`, as MusicBrainz asks.
    - Release-group search in batches of 25 artist IDs (`(arid:… OR …) AND firstreleasedate:[from TO to]`),
      100 per page, every page (safety stop at 100 pages).
    - Name lookup only when certain: score 90 or more and the same normalised name; quotes and backslashes
      are escaped.
    - A 503 is retried up to 5 attempts with waits of 2, 4, 8 and 16 s. Other statuses fail at once.
    - Parsed with `org.json`.
- `NewReleaseMatching`, ported from iOS:
  - `isMusicBrainzId` (UUIDs only).
  - `normalisedTitle`: folds diacritics, spells out ø/æ/ß and similar, drops curly quotes, articles and
    edition furniture.
  - `normalisedName`, `typeLabel`, `isReleased` (partial dates compared as text), `musicBrainzDay`
    (UTC), `date`.
- No dependencies (`org.json` is the platform's), no schema changes, no Koin wiring (B adds it), no UI.
