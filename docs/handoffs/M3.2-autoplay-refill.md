# M3.2 Part D3a: bounded autoplay refill coordinator

**Status: DONE (unwired helper).** Based on b459d11e; initially clean.

## Implementation

- playback/AutoplayRefill.extend(snapshot, position, downloadedOnly) attempts one bounded refill.
  Requires enabled, valid current position, at most 15 upcoming songs, repeat off and shuffle off.
- Uses current song as Instant Mix seed via the of factory. Generator is injectable for tests.
- Reads at most 50 preceding songs plus current/upcoming (at most 66 combined queue rows),
  and 50 recent play events, to exclude nearby/recent duplicates. No full song-table/queue scan.
- Caps generator output to 50 candidates, deduplicates and validates IDs against the mirror;
  downloadedOnly independently filters to completed downloads before append.
- Mutex ignores overlapping attempts. Enabled/source rechecked after generation; D2 atomic append
  rejects stale queue contexts and changed modes. Failure/cancellation propagates; mutex released in finally.
- No schema/dependency changes, Koin registration, controller observer, preference or UI yet.
  Existing Instant Mix factory supplies cached server/local/download behavior when later wired.
  This helper makes no automatic requests today.
