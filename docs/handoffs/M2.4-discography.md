# M2.4 Part F: artist discography split by release type

**Status:** complete (12 Nov 2026). Implemented and verified by Claude Code; committed on its own (see Git).

## What was built

- The artist page's own releases split into shelves, in iOS order: Albums, EPs, Singles, Live albums,
  Compilations, Other releases. Then Appears on. Empty shelves are hidden. Each shelf keeps the page's newest-first order.
- Each shelf header has its icon, the title (tap to fold the shelf away; a chevron shows the state), and Play and
  Shuffle for that shelf. Folding is per page visit (`rememberSaveable`) and everything starts expanded, as on iOS.
- Shelf Play plays the shelf's albums in full, in the listed order, with shuffle off; Shuffle shuffles across them.
- Appears on Play/Shuffle plays only the tracks crediting the artist on other artists' albums, as on iOS.
- The existing hero shuffle (whole artist) is unchanged. The page stays the two-column cover grid.

## Key files and decisions

- `data/model/AlbumReleaseCategory.kt`: a port of iOS `AlbumReleaseCategory`. Words are split on non-letters;
  live beats compilation (or the server's `compilation` flag), which beats EP ("extended play" too), single and the
  "other" secondary types. No types means an album. `List<Album>.releaseShelves()` groups in enum order.
- `LibraryDao.artistReleaseTypes`: release types for the artist's album-credited albums (raw query reusing
  `ALBUM_CREDIT_ALBUM_IDS`). `LibraryRepository.artistAlbums` attaches them to `albumCredits` (the `@Ignore`
  `Album.releaseTypes` field). Appearances are not classified.
- Playback stays reference-based; nothing is copied:
  - `PlaybackController.playAlbums(sourceId, albumIds, shuffled)` queues one `Album` entry per release, so "Playing
    from" names the current album and album pages show their playing indicator.
  - `playArtistAppearances` uses a new `QueueKind.ArtistAppearances`. `QueueDao.artistAppearancesLength`/`Songs` give
    the artist's credited songs whose album exists and is not album-credited to them (the same split the page uses),
    in the page's order.
  - The kind is stored by name, so there is no migration. Its source name and the Now Playing label reuse the artist's.
- UI: `DiscographyHeader` replaces `ArtistSectionHeader` in `ArtistDetailScreen`. Strings: "EPs", "Singles", "Live
  albums", "Compilations", "Other releases", "Play %s", "Shuffle %s", and Expanded/Collapsed state descriptions.
- No schema or dependency change. Release types were already synced in M2.2. Servers that send none (classic
  Subsonic, gonic) show everything under Albums, as on iOS.

## Verification

- `AlbumReleaseCategoryTest`: every iOS test case, the compilation flag, and shelf order and grouping.
- `LibraryRepositoryTest`: album credits carry their own release types (not another artist's album's); Appears on
  plays only the artist's credited track on the guest album. Mutation check: disabling the album-credit exclusion
  failed this test, and restoring it made it pass.
- `PlaybackControllerTest`: a shelf plays its albums in the listed order as Album references, with shuffle turned
  off, and a shelf shuffle stays within its albums.
- `ArtistDetailScreenTest`: Albums and Singles headers, Play and Shuffle callbacks with the right albums and
  mode, folding and unfolding, and Appears on shuffle.
- Final: ktlintFormat, assembleDebug, testDebugUnitTest (1,204 tests, 0 failures), ktlintCheck, lintDebug.
- Pixel 9 API 37 with the real Navidrome mirror, Keith Jarrett:
  - Albums (Facing You … The Melody At Night) and a Live albums shelf (Roma, The Old Country, Bordeaux Concert,
    Munich 2016).
  - Live albums Play started "Roma, Part I"; folding Live albums collapsed it to its header.

## Not verified / limits

- On the emulator I did not check:
  - EP, Single, Compilation or Other shelves (unit tests only).
  - Appears on Play.
  - That shelf playback continues into the next album.
- Offline with real downloads, API 35, TalkBack, 2× font.
- Not run: `queryTiming` with 200k songs (for the Appears on queries and release types).
- Fold state is not persisted across visits (iOS keeps it per view too).
- The scroll estimate that fades the top bar assumes uniform rows. Folded shelves can make the fade start slightly
  early or late, which is cosmetic.
