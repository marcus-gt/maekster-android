**Status: DONE (17 Nov 2026).** Started by Codex (C1–C4), interrupted by a session timeout before
verification; completed and verified by Claude Code (C5). Plan and audit: docs/HOME_DISCOVER_CONSOLIDATION.md.

## Implemented

- Independent Home and Discover layouts in DataStore (`HomeLayout`, new `DiscoverLayout` codec,
  `discover_layout` key). Unknown/duplicate/missing sections are tolerated; new sections are inserted
  after their default predecessor, so saved custom order, hidden and grid choices survive.
- New Home default (iOS hierarchy): Pinned, Your playlists, Queue Builder, Your favorite songs, Your favorite
  albums, Recently played, In heavy rotation, Artist picks, Recently added, Rediscover, Genres; legacy
  Recently/Most played artists and Decades hidden by default. User decision: installs without a saved
  layout switch to these defaults; saved layouts are untouched.
- Queue Builder is a shared `QueueBuilderRoute` placed on Home (online and offline) and Discover; each
  placement has its own ViewModel but observes the server's saved picks continuously.
- Artist picks: up to 4 each of starred, dormant (played > 90 days ago) and never-played credited artists,
  interleaved, day-seeded, all from Room. Partial parity: iOS ranks by play frequency (6/4/2) with adaptive dormancy.
- Genres can be a grid. Labels adapted: Your favorite songs/albums, In heavy rotation, Stations for you.
- Settings > Application: Home sections and Discover sections editors (shared `LayoutEditor`: show/hide,
  drag and accessible reorder, Reset, Save, Cancel) and the Fresh releases consent with its disclosure.
  Settings > Playback: Autoplay similar. Discover has no settings switches. Layout reset never changes consent.
